## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Equalize fraction units | Rewrite quantities so their fractional parts are directly comparable for subtraction by finding a common denominator, creating equivalent fractions, and when needed rewriting a whole number as a whole plus 0 over the relevant denominator. |
| S02 | Normalize number form | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a standard usable subtraction form, such as expressing a whole number with a zero fractional part or converting an improper mixed number to a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Perform the subtraction once quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators of like fractions and subtracting whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers. |
| S04 | Regroup and finalize | Resolve subtraction results that require restructuring by borrowing 1 whole into fractional units and/or simplify and express the result in lowest terms as a fraction, mixed number, or integer. |
