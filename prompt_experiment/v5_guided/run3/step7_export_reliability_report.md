## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Align fraction units | Prepare quantities for subtraction by expressing fractional parts in the same units, including finding a common denominator, rewriting equivalent fractions, or writing a whole number as a whole plus 0 over the needed denominator. |
| S02 | Normalize mixed numbers | Rewrite a mixed number with an improper fractional part as an equivalent standard mixed number before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once quantities are in compatible form by subtracting numerators of like fractions and/or subtracting whole and fractional parts directly when no regrouping is needed. |
| S04 | Regroup and simplify | When direct subtraction is not yet in final form, borrow 1 from the whole part to continue fractional subtraction and/or simplify the resulting fraction or zero form to lowest terms. |
