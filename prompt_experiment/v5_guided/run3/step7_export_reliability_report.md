## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Equalize denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fractions or fractional parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtracting. |

| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out the subtraction once quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators of like fractions and/or subtracting whole and fractional parts of mixed numbers. |
| S04 | Regroup and simplify | Borrow from the whole part when needed to complete the subtraction and express the result in lowest terms or other appropriate final form. |
