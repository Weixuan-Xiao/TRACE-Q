## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as adding a zero fractional part, converting an improper fractional part to a standard mixed number, or separating whole and fractional parts before subtraction. |
| S02 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or both fractional parts as equivalent fractions so they can be subtracted. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with like denominators and/or subtract the whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Regroup or simplify | Borrow one whole when needed to continue subtraction, and/or reduce and express the result in lowest terms or standard final form. |
