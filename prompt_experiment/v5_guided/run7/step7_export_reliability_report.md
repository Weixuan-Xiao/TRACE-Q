## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Align fractional forms | Rewrite quantities into compatible forms for subtraction, including finding common denominators, converting to equivalent fractions, rewriting a whole number with a zero fractional part, and normalizing a mixed number with an improper fractional part. |
| S02 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once the quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators of like-denominator fractions and subtracting whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers when no regrouping is performed in that step. |
| S03 | Borrow across parts | Regroup by taking 1 from the whole-number part and converting it into fractional units so subtraction can continue when the fractional part would otherwise be negative. |
| S04 | Simplify and express result | Reduce the computed difference to lowest terms and present it in an appropriate final form such as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or 0. |
