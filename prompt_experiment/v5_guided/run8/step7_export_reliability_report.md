## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Equalize fraction units | Rewrite quantities into compatible fractional units for subtraction by finding a common denominator and/or expressing a whole-number part as 0 over the needed denominator. |
| S02 | Normalize mixed form | Rewrite a mixed number with an improper fractional part into an equivalent standard whole-plus-proper-fraction form before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once quantities are in compatible form by subtracting like-denominator fractions and/or corresponding whole and fractional parts. |
| S04 | Regroup and simplify | Resolve the subtraction result by borrowing one whole into fractional units when needed and expressing the answer in lowest terms or final integer/fraction/mixed-number form. |
