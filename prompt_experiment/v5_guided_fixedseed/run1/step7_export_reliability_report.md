## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize subtraction form | Rewrite a mixed number or whole number into a subtraction-ready form, including normalizing an improper mixed number or expressing a whole number as a whole plus a zero-fraction part. |
| S02 | Match denominators | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtracting when denominators are unlike. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out the subtraction once quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators over a common denominator and/or subtracting whole and fractional parts separately. |
| S04 | Borrow across mixed number | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to continue subtraction. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce a fractional result to lowest terms or convert a zero-fraction result to a whole number or 0. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole and fractional parts and express the result in final form as a mixed number, fraction, integer, or 0. |
