## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators do not already match. |
| S02 | Normalize number form | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as expressing a whole as a whole plus zero fraction or converting a mixed number with an improper fractional part into a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractional quantities with a shared denominator and subtract any whole-number parts once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction and adjust the whole-number part when the fractional subtraction would otherwise be negative. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms or remove a zero fractional part when simplification is needed after subtraction. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole and fractional parts and express the answer in an appropriate final form such as a mixed number, fraction, or integer. |
