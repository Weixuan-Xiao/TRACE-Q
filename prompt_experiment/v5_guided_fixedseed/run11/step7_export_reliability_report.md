## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when the denominators are unlike. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as a whole plus zero fractional part or a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract whole-number and fractional parts separately once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as fractional units to continue subtraction when the fractional part of the minuend is smaller than the fractional part of the subtrahend. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms or rewrite a zero-valued fraction as 0 or an integer when appropriate. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole-number part and fractional part into the final mixed number, fraction, or integer answer. |
