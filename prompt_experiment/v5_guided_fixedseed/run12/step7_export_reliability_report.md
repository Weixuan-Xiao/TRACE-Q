## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when the denominators do not already match. |
| S02 | Rewrite operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into subtraction-ready mixed-number form, such as adding a zero fractional part or normalizing an improper fractional part. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract whole-number and fractional parts separately once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow in subtraction | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract the fractional part of the subtrahend. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the subtraction result to lowest terms or convert a zero-valued fraction to 0 or an equivalent integer form when appropriate. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts into a single final value in mixed-number, fraction, or integer form. |
