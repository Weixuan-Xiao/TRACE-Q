## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators differ. |
| S02 | Rewrite operands | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into a subtraction-ready whole-plus-fraction form, including normalizing a nonstandard mixed number with an improper fractional part. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a shared denominator and/or subtract whole-number parts once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow one whole and convert it into denominator-sized fractional units when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the subtraction result to lowest terms or simplify a zero fractional part to a simpler whole-number form. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts, or present the result in final acceptable form as a mixed number, fraction, or integer. |
