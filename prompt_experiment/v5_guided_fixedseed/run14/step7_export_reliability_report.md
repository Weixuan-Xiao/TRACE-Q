## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or more fractional parts as equivalent fractions so subtraction can be performed with like denominators. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number, mixed number, or nonstandard mixed number into an equivalent subtraction-ready form, such as adding a zero fractional part, converting an improper fractional part to a standard mixed number, or converting a mixed number to an improper fraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract the whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract directly. |
| S05 | Express final result | Write the computed result in acceptable final form by simplifying to lowest terms and, when needed, combining whole-number and fractional components into a single fraction, mixed number, whole number, or zero. |
