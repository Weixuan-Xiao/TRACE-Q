## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into an equivalent form that makes part-by-part subtraction possible, such as adding a 0-fraction part, converting an improper fractional part to a standard mixed number, or separating whole and fractional parts. |
| S02 | Common denominator conversion | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or both fractional parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract whole-number parts and/or fractional parts once the quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators over a shared denominator. |
| S04 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow 1 from the whole-number part and convert it into fractional units when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract the subtrahend's fractional part. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms or rewrite a zero-valued fraction as 0 after subtraction. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts into the final answer form, including recognizing when the result should be written as a mixed number, pure fraction, integer, or 0. |
