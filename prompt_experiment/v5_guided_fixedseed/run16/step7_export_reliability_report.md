## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or more fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators do not already match. |
| S02 | Normalize number form | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as expressing a whole as a whole plus zero fractional part or converting an improper mixed form to a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract quantities once they are in compatible form, including subtracting numerators over a shared denominator and subtracting whole-number parts in mixed-number problems. |
| S04 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole-number part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to continue subtraction. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the subtraction result to lowest terms or simplify it to a whole number or zero when appropriate. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts, or recognize that only a fraction or whole number remains, to state the final result. |
