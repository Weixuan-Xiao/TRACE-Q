## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite unlike fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize subtraction form | Rewrite a whole number or a mixed number with an improper fractional part into an equivalent subtraction-ready form with standard whole and fractional parts. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned fractions | Subtract fractional parts once they share a denominator by subtracting numerators over the common denominator. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components when solving mixed-number or whole-number subtraction in decomposed form. |
| S05 | Borrow across mixed number | Regroup one whole as a full denominator-sized fraction when the minuend’s fractional part is too small to subtract the subtrahend’s fractional part. |
| S06 | Simplify and express result | Reduce the result to lowest terms and express the final answer in an appropriate form such as a fraction, mixed number, integer, or 0. |
