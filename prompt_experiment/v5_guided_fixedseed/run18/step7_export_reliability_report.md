## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators differ. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as adding a 0-fraction part or converting an improper fractional part to a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out the subtraction once forms are aligned by subtracting like-denominator fractions and/or subtracting the whole-number and fractional parts separately. |
| S04 | Borrow in subtraction | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract. |
| S05 | Finalize result | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms when needed and express it in the appropriate final form as a fraction, mixed number, integer, or 0. |
