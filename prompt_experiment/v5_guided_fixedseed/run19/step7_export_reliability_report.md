## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators are not already aligned. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number, fraction-bearing whole-number expression, or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready form before carrying out the subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned fractions | Subtract fractions once they share a denominator by subtracting numerators and keeping the common denominator. |
| S04 | Subtract mixed parts | Subtract whole-number parts and fractional parts componentwise in mixed-number or whole-number-plus-fraction expressions before any needed regrouping. |
| S05 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction and add it to the fractional part when componentwise subtraction would otherwise leave an insufficient or negative fractional result. |
| S06 | Simplify and express | Reduce the result to lowest terms and present it in an appropriate final form such as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or zero. |
