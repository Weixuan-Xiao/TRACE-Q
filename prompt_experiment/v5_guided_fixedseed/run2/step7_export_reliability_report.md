## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators differ. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number, fraction-only quantity, or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready whole-and-fraction form before subtracting. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract like-denominator fractions and/or subtract whole-number and fractional parts once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as denominator-over-denominator and add it to the fractional part when the fractional subtraction would otherwise be negative. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms or simplify a zero-fraction result to a whole number or integer. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts and express the result in an acceptable final form such as a mixed number, fraction, or integer. |
