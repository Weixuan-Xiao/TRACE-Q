## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready form before carrying out subtraction. |
| S02 | Common denominator conversion | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or both fractional parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract whole-number parts and/or fractional parts once the operands are already in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract directly. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms or rewrite a zero-valued fraction as 0 after subtraction. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts into the final mixed number, fraction, or integer answer. |
