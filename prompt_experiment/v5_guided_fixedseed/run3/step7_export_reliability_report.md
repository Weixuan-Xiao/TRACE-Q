## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Find a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators are not already aligned. |
| S02 | Normalize subtraction form | Rewrite a mixed number or whole number into a subtraction-ready whole-and-fraction form, including normalizing improper mixed numbers and expressing whole numbers with a zero fractional part. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fraction parts with like denominators and/or subtract whole-number parts once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow 1 from the whole-number part and convert it into fractional units when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce a fractional result to lowest terms or convert a zero fractional result to an integer or zero when appropriate. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts and express the result in an acceptable final form such as a mixed number, fraction, integer, or zero. |
