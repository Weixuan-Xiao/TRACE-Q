## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator, typically the least common denominator, and rewrite unlike-denominator fractions or fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize operand form | Rewrite a whole number or mixed number into a subtraction-ready whole-and-fraction form, including adding a zero-fraction part, normalizing a nonstandard mixed number, or explicitly separating whole and fractional parts for computation. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract same-denominator fraction parts and/or whole-number parts once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole | Regroup by taking 1 from the whole-number part and converting it to fractional units when the minuend’s fraction part is too small to subtract. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms or rewrite a zero-fraction or whole-valued result in its simplest form. |
| S06 | Combine final form | Assemble the computed whole-number and fractional parts into the final mixed number, fraction, or integer answer. |
