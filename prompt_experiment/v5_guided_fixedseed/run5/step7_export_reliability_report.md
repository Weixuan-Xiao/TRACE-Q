## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when the denominators differ. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready whole-and-fraction form without changing its value. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract whole-number and fractional parts once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Regroup whole to fraction | Borrow one whole and convert it into fractional units when the minuend’s fractional part is too small to complete the subtraction directly. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms or remove zero-valued fractional parts to express a standard numerical result. |
| S06 | Compose final answer | Assemble the computed whole and fractional parts and state the answer in final mixed-number, fraction, integer, or zero form. |
