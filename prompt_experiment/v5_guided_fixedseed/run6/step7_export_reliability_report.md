## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators differ. |
| S02 | Normalize operand form | Rewrite a whole number or a mixed number with an improper fractional part into a subtraction-ready form with standard whole and fractional parts. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with like denominators and/or subtract the whole-number and fractional parts once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as fractional units of the current denominator when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to complete the subtraction. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction or mixed number to lowest terms, including recognizing when the result simplifies to a whole number or zero. |
