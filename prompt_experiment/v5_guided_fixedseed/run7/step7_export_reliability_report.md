## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when denominators do not already match. |
| S02 | Normalize operand form | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a subtraction-ready form, such as adding a 0-fraction part or converting an improper mixed number to a standard mixed number. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract the whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers once the quantities are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as fractional units to resolve an insufficient or negative fractional part during mixed-number or whole-minus-fraction subtraction. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the resulting fraction or mixed number to lowest terms or convert a zero fractional part into an integer or whole-number result. |
| S06 | Compose final form | Combine the computed whole-number and fractional parts into the final mixed number, fraction, or integer representation when that assembly is shown as a distinct step. |
