## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Match denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite unlike fractional parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite an operand into a subtraction-ready equivalent form, including normalizing a mixed number with an improper fractional part or rewriting a whole number as a whole plus 0 over a needed denominator. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with like denominators and/or subtract the whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers once the forms are aligned. |
| S04 | Borrow in subtraction | Regroup one whole as denominator-over-denominator fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional subtraction would otherwise be negative or insufficient. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce the result to lowest terms and remove any zero fractional part so the answer is expressed in simplest form. |
