## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when the denominators differ. |
| S02 | Normalize operand form | Rewrite a whole number or a mixed number with an improper fractional part into a subtraction-ready form without changing its value. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract fractions with a common denominator and/or subtract the whole and fractional parts of mixed numbers once the operands are in compatible form. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as a fraction with the current denominator and adjust the whole part to complete subtraction when the fractional part would otherwise be negative. |
| S05 | Simplify result | Reduce a fractional result to lowest terms or simplify a zero-fraction expression to an integer or whole-number result. |
