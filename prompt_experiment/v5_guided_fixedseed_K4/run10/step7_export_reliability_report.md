## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Align fractional forms | Rewrite quantities into compatible forms before subtraction by finding a common denominator, converting to equivalent fractions, rewriting a whole number with a 0/d fractional part, and/or normalizing a mixed number whose fractional part is nonstandard. |
| S02 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out the subtraction once forms are compatible, including subtracting numerators of like fractions and subtracting whole-number and fractional parts of mixed numbers before any needed regrouping. |
| S03 | Borrow across parts | Regroup by borrowing 1 from the whole part and converting it into fractional units when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract directly. |
| S04 | Simplify and express | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms and express it appropriately as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or zero. |
