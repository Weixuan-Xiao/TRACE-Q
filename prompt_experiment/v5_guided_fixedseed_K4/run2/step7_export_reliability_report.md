## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Align fraction forms | Rewrite quantities into a directly subtractable form before subtraction by matching denominators, normalizing mixed numbers with improper fractional parts, or rewriting a whole number as a mixed form with a zero fractional part. |
| S02 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once forms are compatible by subtracting numerators of like fractions and/or subtracting whole-number and fractional parts separately. |
| S03 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow 1 whole from the whole-number part and convert it into denominator-sized fractional units so subtraction can continue when the fractional part is insufficient. |
| S04 | Simplify and express | Reduce the result to lowest terms and present it in an appropriate final form such as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or zero. |
