## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Rewrite into compatible form | Rewrite one or both quantities into equivalent subtraction-ready forms before subtracting, including finding common denominators, converting equivalent fractions, normalizing mixed numbers with improper fractional parts, simplifying a fractional part before subtraction, or writing a whole number with a fractional part. |
| S02 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once the quantities are in compatible form, including subtracting like fractions and subtracting whole and fractional parts of mixed numbers when no regrouping is performed in that step. |
| S03 | Borrow across whole | Regroup by taking 1 from the whole-number part and converting it into fractional units so subtraction can continue when the fractional part of the minuend is too small. |
| S04 | Simplify and express result | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms and express it appropriately as a fraction, mixed number, integer, or zero by combining or removing parts as needed. |
