## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fractional parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize subtraction form | Rewrite a quantity into a subtraction-ready mixed-number form, including converting an improper mixed number to standard mixed form or writing a whole number as a whole plus 0 over a denominator. |
| S03 | Subtract fraction parts | Subtract fractions that already share a denominator by subtracting numerators and keeping the denominator, including fraction parts within mixed-number subtraction. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components of mixed numbers or rewritten whole numbers as a separate part of the computation. |
| S05 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as fractional units to continue subtraction when the fractional part of the minuend is too small. |
