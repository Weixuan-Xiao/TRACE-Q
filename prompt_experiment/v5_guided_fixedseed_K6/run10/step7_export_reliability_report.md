## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize given form | Rewrite an operand into a more workable equivalent form before subtraction, such as converting a mixed number with an improper fractional part to standard mixed-number form or simplifying a fraction part within a mixed number. |
| S02 | Decompose whole number | Rewrite a whole number as a whole part plus a zero-valued fractional part so it can be handled in mixed-form subtraction. |
| S03 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions before subtraction when needed. |
| S04 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once forms are aligned, including subtracting numerators of like-denominator fractions and subtracting whole and fractional parts of mixed numbers or whole-number expressions. |
| S05 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to complete the subtraction directly. |
| S06 | Simplify and combine | Express the computed result in lowest terms and in an appropriate final form by simplifying fractions and combining or collapsing whole and fractional parts as needed. |
