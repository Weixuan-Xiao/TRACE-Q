## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite nonzero fraction parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S02 | Rewrite operands | Rewrite an operand into subtraction-ready form by either normalizing a mixed number with an improper fractional part or expressing a whole number as a whole plus zero fraction. |
| S03 | Subtract aligned parts | Subtract once quantities are in compatible form by subtracting numerators over a common denominator and/or subtracting whole-number parts. |
| S04 | Borrow across whole-fraction | Regroup one whole as an equivalent fraction unit and adjust the whole part when the fractional part of the minuend is too small to subtract. |
| S05 | Combine result parts | Assemble the computed whole and fractional components into a single result form before or as part of the final answer. |
| S06 | Simplify fraction result | Reduce a resulting fraction to lowest terms when simplification is required. |
