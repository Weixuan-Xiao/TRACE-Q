## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize given form | Rewrite a nonstandard mixed number or a whole number into a subtraction-ready form, such as converting an improper fractional part into a standard mixed number or expressing a whole number as a whole plus 0 over a needed denominator. |
| S02 | Common denominator conversion | Find a common denominator and rewrite one or both fractions as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract fraction parts | Subtract fractional quantities once they are in compatible form, including same-denominator fraction subtraction and the fractional part of mixed-number subtraction. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components in a mixed-number or whole-number subtraction setup. |
| S05 | Borrow across mixed number | Regroup by taking 1 from the whole-number part and converting it into denominator-sized fractional units when the fractional subtraction would otherwise be negative. |
| S06 | Simplify and combine | Reduce the computed result to lowest terms and express it in an appropriate final form such as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or zero. |
