## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize mixed forms | Rewrite a mixed number with an improper fractional part into an equivalent standard mixed number before subtraction. |
| S02 | Align denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite fraction parts as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S03 | Subtract fraction parts | Subtract fractional quantities once they are in compatible form, including same-denominator fraction subtraction within fraction-only or mixed-number problems. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components of mixed numbers or rewritten whole-plus-fraction forms. |
| S05 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional subtraction would otherwise be negative or insufficient. |
| S06 | Simplify and express | Reduce the result to lowest terms and present it in an appropriate final form such as a fraction, mixed number, whole number, or 0. |
