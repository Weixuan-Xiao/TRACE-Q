## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite unlike fractions as equivalent fractions before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number, mixed number, or fraction into an equivalent subtraction-ready form without changing its value. |
| S03 | Subtract like fractions | Subtract fractions that already share a denominator by subtracting numerators and keeping the denominator, including fractional parts of mixed numbers once aligned. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components of mixed numbers or whole numbers once the operands are in subtraction-ready form. |
| S05 | Borrow across whole | Regroup one whole as fractional units to continue subtraction when the fractional part of the minuend is too small. |
| S06 | Finalize result | Express the subtraction result in correct final form by simplifying to lowest terms when needed and combining whole and fractional parts when applicable. |
