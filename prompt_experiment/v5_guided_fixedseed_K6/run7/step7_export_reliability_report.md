## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Find common denominator | Determine a common denominator and rewrite unlike fractions as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize operand form | Rewrite a starting number into an equivalent, more workable form before subtraction, such as standardizing an improper mixed number or simplifying a starting fraction part. |
| S03 | Align mixed-number parts | Represent whole numbers and mixed numbers in aligned whole-part and fraction-part form so corresponding parts can be subtracted. |
| S04 | Subtract aligned parts | Carry out subtraction once quantities are aligned, including subtracting numerators of like fractions and subtracting whole parts and fraction parts separately in mixed-number problems. |
| S05 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow 1 from the whole-number part and convert it into fractional units when the fractional subtraction cannot be completed directly. |
| S06 | Finalize simplified result | Combine computed whole and fractional parts and express the answer in lowest terms, including reducing to a fraction, mixed number, integer, or zero as appropriate. |
