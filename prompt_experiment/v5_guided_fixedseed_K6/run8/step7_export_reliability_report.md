## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Equalize denominators | Find a common denominator and rewrite unlike fractions as equivalent fractions with the same denominator before subtraction. |
| S02 | Normalize operands | Rewrite a whole number or nonstandard mixed number into a compatible equivalent form for subtraction before the actual subtraction is carried out. |
| S03 | Subtract fractions | Subtract fractional quantities once they are in compatible form, including fraction-only subtraction and subtraction of fractional parts within mixed numbers. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components of mixed numbers or rewritten whole-number expressions as a distinct operation from fractional subtraction. |
| S05 | Regroup for subtraction | Borrow one whole from the whole-number part and convert it into fractional units when the fractional subtraction cannot be completed directly. |
| S06 | Simplify final result | Combine whole and fractional results as needed and express the answer in lowest terms and appropriate final form. |
