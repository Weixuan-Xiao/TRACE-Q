## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Normalize nonstandard forms | Rewrite a nonstandard quantity into an equivalent standard form before subtraction, including normalizing improper fractional parts in mixed numbers and representing a whole number with a zero fractional part when needed. |
| S02 | Create common denominator | Find a common denominator and convert fraction parts to equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtracting. |
| S03 | Subtract fraction parts | Subtract fractional quantities once they are in compatible form, including like fractions and the fractional parts of mixed numbers. |
| S04 | Subtract whole parts | Subtract the whole-number components of the quantities, including whole parts of mixed numbers and subtraction against an implied whole part of zero. |
| S05 | Borrow across parts | Regroup one whole as fractional units and adjust the whole part when the fractional part is too small to subtract directly. |
| S06 | Compose and simplify result | Combine computed whole and fractional parts into the final answer and reduce the result to lowest terms, integer form, or zero when appropriate. |
