## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Common denominator conversion | Determine a least common (or target) denominator and rewrite one or both fractions as equivalent fractions with that common denominator to enable subtraction. |
| S02 | Subtract compatible quantities | Compute the difference once quantities are in compatible form (e.g., like denominators or aligned whole/fraction parts) by subtracting the appropriate parts and forming the resulting value. |
| S03 | Rewrite mixed/whole forms | Rewrite mixed numbers or whole numbers into an equivalent form that makes subtraction straightforward (e.g., mixed→improper, interpret nonstandard mixed numbers, or express a whole number with a chosen denominator/zero fractional part). |
| S04 | Borrow/regroup in subtraction | When a mixed-number (or whole-minus-fraction) subtraction would produce a negative fractional part, borrow 1 from the whole part and add one whole (d/d) to the fractional part, then continue the subtraction. |
