## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Common denominator conversion | Determine a least common denominator (LCD/LCM) and rewrite one or both fractions as equivalent fractions with that common denominator before subtracting. |
| S02 | Subtract like fractions | Subtract fractions with a common denominator by subtracting numerators and keeping the shared denominator (allowing zero or negative numerator differences). |
| S03 | Mixed/whole restructuring | Rewrite mixed numbers or whole numbers into an equivalent form that enables subtraction (e.g., separate whole and fractional parts, convert mixed to improper, or write a whole number as n 0/d). |
| S04 | Regroup and simplify result | When needed, borrow/regroup between whole and fractional parts to avoid a negative fractional part, then express the final answer in simplest form (reduce and/or convert to integer or mixed number). |
