## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Common denominator conversion | Find a least/common denominator (or use a given one) and rewrite one or both fractions as equivalent fractions with the same denominator before subtracting. |
| S02 | Subtract like-denominator fractions | Compute the difference once parts are aligned by subtracting numerators over a common denominator (including as the fractional-part step within mixed-number subtraction). |
| S03 | Mixed-number regrouping | Regroup whole and fractional parts (including borrowing 1 from the whole and adding an equivalent d/d to the fraction part or rewriting a whole as a mixed number) to enable valid subtraction and then recombine. |
| S04 | Simplify and format result | Reduce the resulting fraction to lowest terms and express the final answer in an appropriate form (integer, proper/improper fraction, or mixed number). |
