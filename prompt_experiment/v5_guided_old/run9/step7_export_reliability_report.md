## Skill Codebook

| skill_id | name | definition |
|----------|------|------------|
| S01 | Common denominator conversion | Find a least common denominator (LCD) and rewrite one or both fractions as equivalent fractions with that denominator before subtracting. |
| S02 | Subtract aligned quantities | Subtract once quantities are in compatible form (e.g., same denominator and/or separated whole and fractional parts) by subtracting numerators and/or whole-number parts and combining the result. |
| S03 | Regroup/borrow to subtract | Regroup by borrowing 1 from the whole-number part (or rewriting a whole as a mixed form) to make the fractional subtraction nonnegative, then continue the subtraction. |
| S04 | Simplify to lowest terms | Reduce fractions (including zero fractions) to lowest terms and express the final value in simplest acceptable form (integer, proper/improper fraction, or mixed number). |
